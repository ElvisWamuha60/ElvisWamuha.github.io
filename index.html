Was ich unterrichte · What I teach

Three levels of the Common European Framework (CEFR), each taught with real conversation and exercises.

A1

Anfänger · Beginner

Alphabet and pronunciation, greetings, numbers, introducing yourself, simple present tense and everyday phrases.

A2

Grundlagen · Elementary

Past tense, cases, shopping, directions, daily routines, short messages and simple conversations.

B1

Mittelstufe · Intermediate

Express opinions, longer texts, formal and informal writing, listening practice and exam preparation.
